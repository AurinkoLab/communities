BongaaBirds Stations

  • Environmental sustainability
  • Digital skills

Young people build two low-cost stations that recognise bird calls on the device, then install them and follow the detections on a shared map.

Age
10+
Format
4 lessons
Stations
2

Lessons

  • Lessons 1–3: assemble the stations and test the AI model
  • Lesson 4: install the stations, watch the birds, and analyse the dynamics and which species passed through

The stations listen to birds only: speech is filtered out before any recording is stored.

What the community gets

  • Two station kits: microphone, processing unit, power supply and weatherproof case
  • Guidance for each lesson and access to the bird map
  • Project support from AurinkoLab
Startup cost€1,250two stations

Line-Following Robots Workshop

  • Digital skills
  • Community connection

A 1–2 day workshop with 10 robot kits: participants assemble the robots, program them and compete in timed runs on a track and with robot artwork made for their own city.

Participants
10–30
Event
1–2 days
Kits
10 robots

How it runs

  • One kit per person or per team of up to three
  • Block-based coding in mBlock or C++ with Arduino Wiring
  • Tasks grow from simple turning rules to comparing sensor layouts and P/PD/PID control
  • Local facilitators teach; the host provides a venue, laptops and tools

Proven in Riihimäki, where students designed a city-map track and a bus robot with local partners.

What the community gets

  • Ten Arduino-compatible robot kits and one shared printed track showing your city or region
  • Robot artwork designed for your city or region
  • Spare parts, open-source instructions and code examples
  • Four hours of remote facilitator training and consultation
  • Kits stay with the community
Startup cost€3,800including a city-specific map and robot artwork

How a project starts

  1. Choose a project

    Pick the one that fits your group's age, time and budget.

  2. Form a team

    Gather your participants and a local facilitator or teacher.

  3. Secure funding

    Use the startup cost above when applying to a community fund or grant.

  4. Build and share

    We provide the kit and guidance; you build, test and show the results.
